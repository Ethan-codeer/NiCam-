# Nikon expansion photographs

Ten authentic camera photographs sourced from Wikimedia Commons on 2026-11-06. Each exact model was checked against its file description and visually inspected in the contact sheet. These depict the equipment itself, not example photographs taken with it. Some bodies have a lens attached.

All assets are locally stored as WebP, longest edge 1280 px; aspect ratio preserved, no model substitutions or generated images. Attribution, source URLs, selected licenses, and transformation notes are in `src/photos.json` and `public/photo-credits.json`. The D5600 source offers multiple licenses; CC BY-SA 3.0 was selected.

Visual QA: all ten files opened together in the contact sheet and verified for recognizable camera body, matching model marking/description, readable composition, and absence of broken or placeholder imagery. Contact sheet: `docs/EXPANSION-PHOTOS-contact-sheet.jpg`.
